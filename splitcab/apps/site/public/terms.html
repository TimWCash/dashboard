1. What SplitCab is

SplitCab matches people who want to share a taxi. We are not a taxi operator or transport provider, we don't dispatch vehicles, and drivers don't work for us. You book a licensed taxi yourselves, through a taxi app or at a rank, and the taxi operator's terms apply to the ride.

2. Who can use it

You must be 18 or over, live in or be visiting Ireland, and give a mobile number you control. One account per person. Trips must start and end in the Republic of Ireland.

3. The match fee

  • When you post a trip we place a hold of €1.99 on your card. You can plan a trip up to 60 days ahead: for a trip more than 6 days away, we save it and place the hold 6 days before, when we start looking.
  • It's charged only when you and your match both accept.
  • If no one is matched in time, or you cancel before both of you accept, the hold is released.
  • If your match cancels after you've both accepted, your fee is refunded. If you cancel after both accepting, it isn't. Once the pickup time has passed, the ride can't be cancelled in the app; report any problem from Safety.
  • If you block someone for safety reasons after accepting, both fees are refunded.

Your right to cancel. Consumer law gives you 14 days to cancel a service you buy online. Each time you post a trip, the app asks you to confirm that you want us to start looking straight away (or, for a trip planned more than 6 days ahead, as soon as the search opens), and that the matching service is complete once you and your match both accept. Until then you can cancel for free and the hold is released. Once you've both accepted, the 14-day right to cancel no longer applies to that fee (Consumer Rights Act 2022). We record when you confirmed.

4. Sharing the fare

Before you accept, we show an estimate of each person's share based on the National Transport Authority's maximum fares. After the ride, the person who booked enters the meter total and each share is adjusted in the same proportion. If they haven't entered it 6 hours after the ride, we use the estimate you both accepted; they can still correct it until the other traveller has paid. The other traveller pays their share directly to the booker, for example through the booker's Revolut page or in cash. Payments through Revolut are between you, Revolut and the booker, under Revolut's terms. We don't collect or guarantee fare payments between travellers, but we act on reports of non-payment, including closing accounts.

We filter chat, review every report, and may suspend or close accounts that break these rules. If more than one person reports someone, we pause their account straight away while we look into it, and tell them how to contact us. Report anyone who makes you feel unsafe; in an emergency call 112 or 999.
