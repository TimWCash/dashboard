In danger? Call 112 or 999 now. Then report the person in the app: open the trip and tap Safety.

Was I charged?

We place a €1.99 hold when you post a trip. It's only charged if you're matched and you both accept. Otherwise it's released automatically, though your bank may show it as pending for a few days.

My travel partner didn't pay their share

Open the trip, tap Safety and choose "They didn't pay their share". Our team reviews every report.

Someone cancelled after we both accepted

If your travel partner cancels, your match fee is refunded and we look for someone else. If you cancel after accepting, your fee isn't refunded.
