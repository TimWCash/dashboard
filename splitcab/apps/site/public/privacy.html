This policy explains what personal data SplitCab collects, why, and your rights. It covers the SplitCab app and this website. Tim Cashman runs SplitCab and is the data controller. Contact us at timothy.cashman@gmail.com.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we don't sell your data.

Who sees your data

Your travel partner sees your first name, rating and an approximate pickup area when you're matched, and your exact pickup point, messages and share of the fare once you've both accepted. Your phone number is never shown.

Service providers process data for us under contract: Stripe (payments), Twilio (sign-in codes), Expo (push notifications), Google (address search, and the trip map on Android), Apple (the trip map on iPhone) and Vercel and Supabase (hosting, with our servers and database in Ireland). Some are based in the United States; transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

We share data with the Gardaí or other authorities only when the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

This website and the demo

This website doesn't use cookies, forms or tracking, and doesn't load anything from other sites. Our host, Vercel, processes your IP address and the pages you request in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse (legitimate interests).

The demo keeps less data, for a day, and has its own privacy notice.

How long we keep it

These limits are enforced automatically.

  • Chat messages: 90 days.
  • Sign-in code requests: 1 day. Expired sign-in sessions: 7 days.
  • Exact pickup and drop-off places: 1 year. After that we keep only the rough area (for example "Trinity College area").
  • Details you write in a safety report: 2 years.
  • Account details: until you delete your account. If you don't use SplitCab for 2 years, we delete your account.
  • Trip, payment and receipt records: 6 years, as Irish tax law requires, then deleted. When you delete your account we remove your name, number and place names straight away, and blur locations to roughly the nearest kilometre.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Most of this is built into the app under Account → Privacy & your data: download everything we hold as a file, correct your name, remove your Revolut username, and delete your account. For anything else, email us; we reply within a month. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

A download of your data doesn't include reports other people made about you, to protect the people who made them.

Age

SplitCab is only for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

We'll tell you in the app before any significant change to this policy takes effect, and ask you to read it before your next trip. We record which version you agreed to.
