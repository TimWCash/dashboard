Share the taxi. Split the fare.

SplitCab matches you with someone heading the same way at roughly the same time, so you can share one taxi and pay less. No match, no charge.

How it works

  1. Tell us where you're going and roughly when.
  2. We find someone nearby going your way and show what you'll each pay.
  3. You both accept. One of you books the taxi; the other pays their share to the booker after the ride.

The €1.99 match fee is only charged if you're matched and you both accept.
