Delete your account

You can delete your SplitCab account at any time.

In the app

Open SplitCab, go to Account → Delete account and confirm. It takes effect straight away.

Without the app

Email [support@your-domain.ie] from any address with the subject "Delete my account" and the mobile number you signed up with. We'll send a text to that number to confirm it's you, then delete the account within 30 days (usually much sooner) and email you when it's done.

What's deleted and what's kept

Deleted immediatelyYour name, phone number, saved card details, notification token and sign-in sessions. Any open trips are cancelled.
Kept, without your name or numberTrip and payment records, for accounting and to handle disputes, for up to 6 years as Irish tax law requires.
Deleted after 90 daysChat messages (this applies to everyone, not just deleted accounts).
