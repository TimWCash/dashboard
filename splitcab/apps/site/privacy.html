Who sees your data

Your travel partner sees your first name, rating and an approximate pickup area when you're matched, and your exact pickup point, messages and share of the fare once you've both accepted. Your phone number is never shown.

Service providers process data for us under contract: Stripe (payments), Twilio (sign-in codes), Expo (push notifications), Google (address search) and [hosting provider] (servers in the EU). Some are based in the United States; transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

We share data with the Gardaí or other authorities only when the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

  • Chat messages: 90 days.
  • Sign-in code requests: 1 day. Expired sign-in sessions: 7 days.
  • Account details: until you delete your account.
  • Trip and payment records: up to 6 years, as Irish tax law requires. When you delete your account we remove your name, number and place names, and blur locations to roughly the nearest kilometre.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Use Account → Download my data and Account → Delete account in the app, or email us. You can complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

Age

SplitCab is only for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

We'll tell you in the app before any significant change to this policy takes effect.
